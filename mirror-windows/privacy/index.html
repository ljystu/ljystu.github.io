Mirror Windows — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Mirror Windows is a browser extension that shows the same tabs in every Chrome window.

What it handles. To do this, the extension reads the address, title, icon and pinned state of your open tabs and the names and colours of your tab groups, and, only if you turn on page previews, takes a picture of the visible page when it moves to another window and while another window is standing in for it, so those windows can show a blurred preview.

Where it goes. Nowhere. All of this stays on your computer, in the extension's own browser storage. The page pictures are kept only in chrome.storage.session, which Chrome clears when the browser closes; they are also deleted when you turn mirroring or page previews off. To bring the same tabs back after a restart, chrome.storage.local keeps the address, title and pinned state of each open tab, the names and colours of your tab groups, the on/off setting and your settings (such as the sites never to preview). The extension makes no network requests, has no server, no analytics and no ads, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Removing it. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

Mirror Windows 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-31

Mirror Windows 让所有 Chrome 窗口显示同一组标签页。为此，扩展会读取你打开的标签页的网址、标题、图标和是否固定，以及标签组的名称和颜色；只有在你开启页面预览后，才会在页面移到别的窗口时、以及另一个窗口在替它占位时，给可见页面截图，用来在那个窗口里显示模糊预览。

这些数据只保存在你自己电脑上的扩展存储里。页面截图只放在 chrome.storage.session，关闭浏览器时由 Chrome 清空，关闭同步或关闭预览时也会删除；为了重启后恢复同样的标签，chrome.storage.local 保存每个打开标签的网址、标题和是否固定、标签组的名称和颜色，以及开关和设置。扩展不联网，没有服务器、统计或广告，不出售、不共享、不传输任何数据。卸载扩展即删除它存的所有内容。
